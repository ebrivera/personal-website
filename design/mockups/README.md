# Redesign mockups

Static HTML mockups for the ebrivera.com redesign. Open a file directly in a browser; nothing here is part of the Next.js build.

- `round-4.html`: the chosen direction. A calm one-column home (name, blurb, welcome, nav, experience, contacts). Clicking a nav item slides the column into a skinny left sidebar while the page slides in from the right. Thin Spotify-style bar at the bottom. Light palettes only. Dashed `write:` boxes are copy still to be written.
- `round-5-variations.html`: round 4 with only fonts and colors varied (switcher at top right is for review only), plus a mock "add to my queue" button in the Spotify bar.
- `chosen.html`: the locked direction. Round 4 layout in Fenway green (#54796D background, cream text) with Libre Franklin, filled with Ernesto's own copy. About has a "show and tell" toy chest: click a sticker and it pops out, then turns over to a note on the back (places map, coffee, listening, training, photos). `p/receiver.jpg` is a duotoned 1971 Pioneer SX-2500 ad scan by Nesster (CC BY 2.0).
